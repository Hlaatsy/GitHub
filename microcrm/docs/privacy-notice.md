# Privacy notice template

Fill in the [brackets], publish it at an https address (your website, or a public document), and set `PRIVACY_NOTICE_URL` to that address. Customers see the link in their first WhatsApp menu.

This is a starting point, not legal advice. Have it checked if your business handles anything sensitive.

---

## How [Business name] uses your information

When you message **[Business name]** on WhatsApp ([business WhatsApp number]), we collect and use your information as set out below, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

**Who is responsible.** [Business name], [physical address], [email]. Our WhatsApp ordering system is run for us by [platform operator name], who processes your information only on our instructions and keeps it secure.

**What we collect**
- Your WhatsApp number and profile name
- What you book or order, and when
- Your delivery address, if you choose delivery (we remember it for your next order)
- Payment status. Card and bank details go directly to [PayFast / Paystack] and are never seen or stored by us

We do not store the content of your messages, only that a message was sent and when.

**Why.** To take your booking or order, collect payment, deliver or arrange collection, and contact you about it. We do not send marketing without your consent, and we do not sell your information.

**Who we share it with**
- Meta (WhatsApp), to deliver messages
- [PayFast / Paystack], to process your payment
- [Courier name], for deliveries, with your name, number and address only

**How long we keep it.** Your contact details and saved address are kept until you ask us to delete them. Records of orders and payments are kept for five years, because tax law requires it, but they are no longer linked to your name or number once you ask us to delete your information.

**Your rights.** You can ask us:
- what information we hold about you;
- to correct it;
- to delete it.

Message us on WhatsApp or email [email]. Send **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages from us.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator: [inforeg.org.za](https://inforeg.org.za).
