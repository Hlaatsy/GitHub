"How long does it take to make an AI avatar video?"

Four steps with IDENTICAL. Here is the whole workflow.

1. START
Upload a video or a photo, record your voice, or describe your identical twin
in plain text.

2. CUSTOMISE
Choose an avatar from the library or create your own twin, then pick a voice —
text-to-speech, or a recording of your own.

3. CREATE
The AI animates your twin with realistic expressions and movement, speaking
whatever you wrote.

4. SHARE
Export to social media, your website, or straight to your team.

The part that surprises most people: step 1 can be a single photo. No filming
day required to get started.

Which step do you expect to spend the most time on? In practice, it is usually
writing the script — which is exactly where your time should go.
